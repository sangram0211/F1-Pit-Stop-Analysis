# 🏎️ Formula 1 Pit Stop Analysis

A comprehensive data analysis of **Formula 1 pit stops** exploring performance trends, team efficiency, and the impact of pit stop durations on race outcomes.

This project analyzes historical F1 data to understand whether optimizing pit stops significantly affects race performance.

---

# 📊 Project Overview

Pit stops are one of the most exciting and strategic elements of Formula 1 racing. This project explores:

* Pit stop duration trends over time
* Team-wise pit stop performance
* Pit stop consistency and variability
* Fastest pit stop comparisons
* Pit stop contribution to total race time
* Evolution of pit stop performance since 2013

---

# 📈 Key Analysis Performed

## 1. Pit Stop Duration Over Time

* Analyzed how pit stop duration evolved over years
* Observed performance stabilization after 2013

## 2. Team Performance Comparison

Compared teams based on:

* Average pit stop time
* Consistency
* Fastest stops

Top Teams Identified:

* Red Bull
* Mercedes
* Ferrari
* McLaren

---

## 3. Pit Stop Impact on Race

Findings:

* Pit stops account for **~0.83% of total race time**
* Optimization has minimal race impact
* Teams benefit more from car performance and strategy

---

## 4. Variability Analysis

* Standard deviation analysis
* Consistency comparison across constructors

# 📌 Key Conclusions

* Pit stops play an important strategic role
* However, optimizing pit stop time yields limited gains
* Pit stops account for **less than 1%** of total race time
* Focus areas for improvement:

  * Car performance
  * Tire strategy
  * Race pace
